Quantum PDE Solver: HHL vs VQLS Comparison Report

Executive Summary

This report benchmarks Harrow-Hassidim-Lloyd (HHL) against Variational Quantum Linear Solvers (VQLS) for solving 1D frequency-domain Helmholtz equations and seismic Full Waveform Inversion (FWI) linear systems. All experiments use PennyLane and system NumPy solvers under the qpdesolver framework.
